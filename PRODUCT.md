# Engage — Product Scope

Engage is Fusion Party Australia's member policy library, discussion, and decision platform (`Fusion-Party-Aus/Engage`).

## Core Loop

1. **Browse & Read**: Any visitor or member can read the 76 baseline policies and new proposals in plain English (`statement`, `outcomes`, and `details`), organised by topic and stage (`Open for discussion`, `Open for voting`, `Adopted`).
2. **Sign In & Verify**: Members sign in using a one-click email link (Resend) or Google OAuth via Better Auth. Administrators verify party membership to enable commenting and voting.
3. **Comment & Vote Inline**: Verified members post comments or suggested wording amendments and cast `Approve` or `Reject` votes directly on each policy page.
4. **Administer**: Staff and administrators manage policies, change proposal stages, and verify member accounts from a unified administrative workspace.
