# Horizon design system use

This application follows Fusion's established Horizon system. The source of truth is the Horizon repository's `DESIGN.md` and the reusable Astro components in `frontend/src/components` and `frontend/src/layouts`.

- Use a light editorial canvas, deep violet (`#32105A`) for brand foundations, pale lavender and white for reading surfaces, and cyan (`#00C9D2`) for the national accent.
- Use the canonical Horizon emblem, editorial Fraunces headlines, Outfit interface and body text, sweeping ribbon geometry, image-led policy summaries, and the established white navigation/footer.
- Keep policy reading comfortable and uncluttered. Consultations and administrative tasks use the same typography, colour roles, focus styles, and action shapes with denser task layouts where needed.
- Clearly distinguish public policies, consultations, draft ballots, published decisions, and synthetic preview activity by labels and page structure.
- Preserve semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, high contrast, reduced-motion support, and narrow-screen reading order.

The Horizon design brief governs shared assets and components; do not introduce an independent app theme.
